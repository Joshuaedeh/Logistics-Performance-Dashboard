# Logistics Performance Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze logistics operations and provide insights into fleet efficiency, driver performance, delivery performance, maintenance activities, safety incidents, and customer operations.

The project transforms operational data into actionable insights that support better decision-making and performance monitoring

## Project Objectives
- Monitor overall logistics and delivery performance.
- Evaluate fleet utilization and operational efficiency.
- Analyze driver productivity and performance
- Monitor maintenance activities and safety incidents.
- Identify delivery delays and detention patterns.
- Examine customer and facility performance.
- Analyze trip, route, fuel, and distance metrics.

## Dashboard Pages
The dashboard consists of seven interactive pages:
- Executive Overview: High-level operational KPIs and performance summary.
- Delivery Operations: Delivery efficiency, on-time performance, and detention analysis.
- Fleet Performance: Fleet utilization and vehicle performance.
- Driver Performance: Driver productivity, performance metrics, and operational contribution.
- Maintenance & Safety: Maintenance activities and safety incident analysis.
- Customer & Facility: Customer performance and facility-level insights.
- Trip & Route Analysis: Trip activity, distance, and fuel consumption analysis.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query: Data cleaning and transformation
- DAX: Calculated measures and KPI development
- Data Modeling: Relationships between operational datasets.
